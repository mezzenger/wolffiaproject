A minimal privacy footprint

This first version does not collect contact submissions.

The site is static and currently has no contact form, account system, analytics, advertising, or third-party embeds.

What this draft does

  • Serves informational pages and a local SVG mark.
  • Uses a small local script for the mobile menu and current year.
  • Links to external sources when a visitor chooses to follow a source.

What it does not yet do

This draft does not ask for names, email addresses, messages, photos, recipe submissions, donations, or other personal information. Do not add a form or analytics service until the project approves the destination, data practices, retention period, security expectations, and privacy notice.

Future updates

If the site later collects information or uses a service that places cookies or sends data to another provider, this page must be replaced or expanded before that feature goes live.
