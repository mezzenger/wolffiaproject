From water to table, with care

Recipes are a bridge between knowledge and practice.

The recipe section will connect the science question to food culture without pretending that an unapproved crop is ready to serve.

Recipe Atlas integration: The related recipe site exists locally, but its public URL, content permissions, and review status are not yet confirmed.

What belongs here

  • Traditional recipes with accurate attribution and contributor consent.
  • Adapted recipes clearly labeled as adaptations.
  • Experimental recipes clearly separated from established food traditions.
  • Ingredient, allergen, preparation, and food-safety notes reviewed for the intended audience.
  • Sources and permission records for every contributed recipe and image.

What does not belong here yet

This first version does not invite anyone to taste, harvest, or prepare project-grown material. Recipes will be connected only after the relevant species, source, processing, safety, legal, and liability boundaries are approved.

Respectful collaboration

Food knowledge is not an anonymous content mine. Contributors should be named or credited as they prefer, permissions should be documented, and cultural context should be preserved rather than reduced to decoration.

Ask about contributing

Planned content model

Each recipe should record its status, source, contributor, permission, ingredients, preparation notes, cultural context, and review date.
