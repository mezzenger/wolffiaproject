# Phase 2.1 publication-readiness and provenance review

**Review date:** 2026-10-12

**Reviewed base:** `origin/main` at `cd45c8bb45a86876a4cc7131ae0a983108c800e9`

**Decision:** Publication approval is **not established**. This is a desk audit, not a scientific, legal, food-safety, cultural, copyright, or accessibility sign-off.

## Boundary and inventory

The tracked site has 13 HTML pages: home, mission, why aquatic foods, Wolffia, water spinach, responsible growing, pilot project, recipes gateway, community, contact, sources, accessibility, and privacy. Its only tracked image file is `public/wolffia-public-site.svg`; the home illustration is CSS. The recipe gateway is a placeholder. There are no tracked recipe detail pages, photographs, production records, or pilot results. `public/` also contains shared CSS/JS, `robots.txt`, and `_headers`.

The original checkout had **56 uncommitted files** at the start of this review. They include 20 structured recipe drafts in `public/recipe-data/recipes.json`, recipe routes and scripts, `public/images/recipe-hero.webp`, contribution forms, and content/register edits. A SHA-256 manifest was recorded outside the repository before the isolated worktree was created. Those files were inspected read-only, remain in the original checkout, are not in this branch, and are not on the current Pages site. Their editorial labels (`Traditional-Inspired`, `Community Adaptation`, `Test Kitchen Recipe`, `Open-Licensed Research Recipe`) are not the project's four publication statuses.

The current [GitHub Pages home](https://mezzenger.github.io/wolffiaproject/) and selected tracked routes return HTTP 200. The local recipe detail route and an internal register return 404. `robots.txt` says `Disallow: /`, but that does **not** restrict access: draft public pages are already reachable by direct URL. The sampled Pages responses did not contain a Content-Security-Policy or X-Robots-Tag header. GitHub Pages does not apply `_headers`. The live artifact therefore exposes the public draft with its `needs-review` and `placeholder` copy, though not the local recipe files.

## Recipe and media provenance detail

The tracked `public/recipes.html` is only a gateway. The local draft's two research adaptations identify [Seephua et al. (2025)](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/40238231/) and [On-Nom et al. (2023)](https://www.mdpi.com/2304-8158/12/14/2647) and include author credit, source URLs, CC BY URLs, and adaptation notes. [MDPI's rights policy](https://www.mdpi.com/authors/rights) says its articles are generally CC BY 4.0. Article-specific license displays and the accuracy of each conversion/adaptation were not fully checked here; MDPI's article pages returned HTTP 429 to the review tool. Source licensing also does not establish ownership of the atlas's new wording, photos, cultural descriptions, or food-safety accuracy. The other 18 entries have no item-level `sourceUrl`, `licenseUrl`, or `attribution` fields; a default license string appears at render time. Nine entries include cultural-respect notes, but no community reviewer or consent record was found.

The tracked SVG mark has no creator or license record in the register. The CSS pond art is labeled conceptual and has no external asset. The candidate Commons photograph is outside the artifact. The local WebP hero image is not live and remains `do-not-publish` pending provenance. No photographs, private pilot data, exact pilot locations, partner endorsements, or nutritional test results were found in the tracked public artifact.

## Verification and limits

- Ran `./tests/check-all.sh` in the isolated worktree; it checks source, builds `dist/`, and validates the production artifact. Passing confirms structure and links, not content approval.
- Compared the live route status for home, Wolffia, water spinach, recipes, privacy, robots, one local-only recipe route, and an internal register. This was a sample of live routes, not a complete crawl.
- Reviewed the current primary sources linked above on 2026-10-12. The APHIS concurrence matrix is a March 2025 working version; current agency confirmation remains essential. The EFSA opinion concerns a specific powder, not fresh plants or project-grown material. EU findings are not U.S. authorization.
- Inspected uncommitted local recipe metadata and media read-only. No contributor contracts, image generation records, original PNG, lab results, permit files, or owner approval were available in the tracked branch. Absence here does not prove they do not exist elsewhere.
- No expert scientific/medical/legal opinion, culinary tasting, copyright clearance, laboratory test, screen-reader test, or manual accessibility review was performed.

Then assign named scientific/food-safety, regulatory, rights, cultural, and owner reviewers to resolve the P0 records before proposing any recipe or cultivation content for publication.
