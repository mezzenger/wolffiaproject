Everyone belongs here

Accessibility is a design requirement, not a later polish step.

This first version uses semantic HTML, plain language, visible focus styles, responsive layout, keyboard-accessible navigation, and reduced-motion support.

Current commitments

  • Use landmarks, one clear page heading, and logical heading order.
  • Provide a skip link and visible keyboard focus indicators.
  • Keep text and controls readable at narrow widths and larger zoom.
  • Use contrast that does not depend on color alone.
  • Respect prefers-reduced-motion.
  • Keep links descriptive and avoid unexplained jargon.
  • Provide useful alternative text for meaningful images and label decorative images appropriately.
  • Review forms, transcripts, captions, touch targets, and screen-reader behavior before launch.

Known review items

A human review is still needed at multiple zoom levels, with keyboard-only navigation, a screen reader, and on a mobile device. If a future contact form, video, recipe image, or data visualization is added, its accessible name, instructions, error state, alternatives, and consent treatment must be reviewed too.

Feedback route

A public accessibility contact route will be added when the project approves its contact destination and privacy treatment. Until then, this page records the commitment without inventing an address.

Target

Use current WCAG 2.2 AA practices as a review baseline before public launch.

The detailed launch checklist is maintained with the project materials.
