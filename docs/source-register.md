Rules for future entries:

- Prefer current government, regulatory, institutional, or peer-reviewed primary sources.
- Record the exact public claim supported and its limits.
- Do not turn a study of one species, strain, product, or condition into a universal claim.
- Record reviewer, review date, and expiry/recheck date for legal material.
