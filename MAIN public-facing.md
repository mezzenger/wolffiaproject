IMPORTANT REGULATORY / RESPONSIBLE CULTIVATION NOTE

Water spinach, Ipomoea aquatica, is regulated in some places, including Virginia, where it is listed as a Tier 2 noxious weed. It is also listed federally as a noxious weed.

Therefore:
- Do not encourage uncontrolled outdoor planting.
- Do not encourage release into ponds, creeks, rivers, drainage ditches, wetlands, or natural waterways.
- Do not make casual propagation sound harmless.
- Do not write “grow this anywhere.”
- Do frame the project as contained, regulation-aware, responsible, educational, and food-focused.

Use language like:

“Because some aquatic plants can become invasive or regulated outside controlled settings, this project emphasizes contained systems, responsible sourcing, local compliance, and no release into natural waterways.”
