A careful first step

Growing food security from water, wisdom, and community.

The Wolffia Project is exploring aquatic foods as one practical tool for local food resilience. We are beginning with a contained pilot, honest measurements, and respect for safety, culture, and place.

What is Wolffia?

A genus of minute floating flowering plants, sometimes called watermeal. It is not algae.

Our approach

Small plant. Serious mission.

Fast growth does not automatically mean safe food. We will identify what we are growing, control the system, keep records, and treat a pause or stop decision as a responsible result.

A community invitation

Hope needs evidence, and evidence needs people.

We are not asking anyone to bet the farm. We are asking trusted partners to help evaluate one careful, contained pilot: a place to learn, document, teach, and decide whether the model deserves a next step.
